Drawrassic Park – Privacy Policy

Applies to the Drawrassic Park app for iPad. Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Short version

We don't collect any information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game works without an internet connection.

What stays on the iPad

Your park, dinosaur drawings, settings and progress are saved only on the iPad, inside the app. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes this data, so copy the in-game backup code first if you want to keep it.

Apple services the player can choose to use

Photos: the app adds a picture to the photo library only when the player taps to save it. It never reads the library. Camera / photo picker: used only when the player chooses to bring a photo of a drawing into the game; the picture stays on the iPad. iCloud and Game Center (if available in this version): backups and achievements are handled by Apple under the player's own Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive this data.

Children

The game is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.

Contact

Questions: use the "App Support" link on the App Store page.
