# 疯狂山羊 Goat Mayhem (goat-mayhem) — changelog

Folder layout: `index.html` is the version launcher, `versions.json` lists versions, each version is `goat-mayhem-vN.html` (frozen once published). The game loads three.js r160 from `../vendor/three-0.160.min.js` (CDN fallback) and cloud backup from `../shared/family-cloud.js`.

- **v1** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v1.html` · save schema **s1** (key `goat-mayhem/save/s1`, backup `…/bak`, daily copies `…/day-YYYYMMDD` ×7). First version: 3D open-world town (plaza, water tower, skyscraper, parking lot, gas station, construction crane, pond, trampolines, soccer goal, bowling, skate park, farm with barn/silo/windmill/hedge maze, forest treehouse, funfair with goat cannon, bouncy castle and ferris wheel). Headbutt, lick-and-drag/throw, jump + flips, flop (ragdoll), bleat, 8 prank powers, 9 goat skins, 17 gear items, 22 golden bells, 34 tasks, combo chaos score, Chaos Rush (timed). Shared settings `goat-mayhem/settings`, presets `goat-mayhem/presets`, dev config `goat-mayhem/devcfg`, backup time `goat-mayhem/meta`.
- **v2** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v2.html` · save schema **s1** (unchanged, shared with v1). Graphics and feel upgrade: one procedural texture atlas (facades with shop fronts, awnings, balconies, rooftop tanks/AC/solar; paving, cobbles, asphalt, crosswalks; bump mapping; lit windows at night), ambient-occlusion shading, clouds, 9k grass tufts, flowers, bushes, new trees and lamps with light pools; bloom glow; sprite fire/smoke explosions, scorch marks, burning wrecks. New sculpted goat: one seamless skinned body generated at load from blended shapes (about 44k triangles), coloured per region with cavity shading, covered by short hair shells (6 layers; off on Low quality; dev panel: hair layers/length), smooth tube horns with growth ridges, beard, horizontal pupils, blinking lids, lop ears; ears and neck on springs, squash and stretch, headbutt wind-up, hit-stop, sagging tongue. `?quality=low|auto|high` URL override for slow machines (not saved). Animated people from the KayKit CC0 mannequin and clips (`vendor/kaykit/`) in 15 jobs with clothes, hair, hats and gear, plus get-up, punch, fishing, push-ups; Quaternius CC0 cows, horses, alpacas, donkeys (`vendor/quaternius/`). Rounded cars. The page now loads three.js r160 as ES modules (import map) with GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and the bloom passes vendored under `vendor/three-addons-0.160/`.
- **v3** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v3.html` · save schema **s1** (unchanged, shared with v1–v2). Flicker and stutter fixes. Flicker: the glow (bloom) path now renders into a 4× MSAA buffer (v2 had no anti-aliasing whenever glow was on); the shadow light turns in 0.6° steps and its centre snaps to shadow-map texels in light space (v2 moved it every frame, so shadow edges crawled); goat hair shells fade out where a hair is smaller than a pixel; pink inner ears are part of the goat mesh (v2 overlay z-fought); tiny head parts don't receive shadows; camera near plane 0.1→0.2 m. Stutter: every material is compiled while loading, for both screen and glow buffers (v2 compiled people's clothing shaders the first time someone walked near); bloom buffers resize only when the size changes (v2 called setSize every frame); shadow map size fixed at 2048 (v2 reallocated it when resolution dropped); auto-resolution waits 20 s after a drop and raises only after 10 s of fast frames; people and animals hold a new animation 0.15 s before switching (no walk/run/idle flip-flopping); people keep their animated rig a little longer (no popping); camera collision pulls in fast and eases out; headbutt freeze (hit-stop) 75 → 40 ms. The version badge now shows the right number (v2 showed "v1"). Dev panel: shadow turn step, headbutt freeze.
