# 疯狂山羊 Goat Mayhem (goat-mayhem) — changelog

Folder layout: `index.html` is the version launcher, `versions.json` lists versions, each version is `goat-mayhem-vN.html` (frozen once published). The game loads three.js r160 from `../vendor/three-0.160.min.js` (CDN fallback) and cloud backup from `../shared/family-cloud.js`.

- **v1** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v1.html` · save schema **s1** (key `goat-mayhem/save/s1`, backup `…/bak`, daily copies `…/day-YYYYMMDD` ×7). First version: 3D open-world town (plaza, water tower, skyscraper, parking lot, gas station, construction crane, pond, trampolines, soccer goal, bowling, skate park, farm with barn/silo/windmill/hedge maze, forest treehouse, funfair with goat cannon, bouncy castle and ferris wheel). Headbutt, lick-and-drag/throw, jump + flips, flop (ragdoll), bleat, 8 prank powers, 9 goat skins, 17 gear items, 22 golden bells, 34 tasks, combo chaos score, Chaos Rush (timed). Shared settings `goat-mayhem/settings`, presets `goat-mayhem/presets`, dev config `goat-mayhem/devcfg`, backup time `goat-mayhem/meta`.
- **v2** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v2.html` · save schema **s1** (unchanged, shared with v1). Graphics and feel upgrade: one procedural texture atlas (facades with shop fronts, awnings, balconies, rooftop tanks/AC/solar; paving, cobbles, asphalt, crosswalks; bump mapping; lit windows at night), ambient-occlusion shading, clouds, 9k grass tufts, flowers, bushes, new trees and lamps with light pools; bloom glow; sprite fire/smoke explosions, scorch marks, burning wrecks. New sculpted goat: one seamless skinned body generated at load from blended shapes (about 44k triangles), coloured per region with cavity shading, covered by short hair shells (6 layers; off on Low quality; dev panel: hair layers/length), smooth tube horns with growth ridges, beard, horizontal pupils, blinking lids, lop ears; ears and neck on springs, squash and stretch, headbutt wind-up, hit-stop, sagging tongue. `?quality=low|auto|high` URL override for slow machines (not saved). Animated people from the KayKit CC0 mannequin and clips (`vendor/kaykit/`) in 15 jobs with clothes, hair, hats and gear, plus get-up, punch, fishing, push-ups; Quaternius CC0 cows, horses, alpacas, donkeys (`vendor/quaternius/`). Rounded cars. The page now loads three.js r160 as ES modules (import map) with GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and the bloom passes vendored under `vendor/three-addons-0.160/`.
- **v3** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v3.html` · save schema **s1** (unchanged, shared with v1–v2). Flicker and stutter fixes. Flicker: the glow (bloom) path now renders into a 4× MSAA buffer (v2 had no anti-aliasing whenever glow was on); the shadow light turns in 0.6° steps and its centre snaps to shadow-map texels in light space (v2 moved it every frame, so shadow edges crawled); goat hair shells fade out where a hair is smaller than a pixel; pink inner ears are part of the goat mesh (v2 overlay z-fought); tiny head parts don't receive shadows; camera near plane 0.1→0.2 m. Stutter: every material is compiled while loading, for both screen and glow buffers (v2 compiled people's clothing shaders the first time someone walked near); bloom buffers resize only when the size changes (v2 called setSize every frame); shadow map size fixed at 2048 (v2 reallocated it when resolution dropped); auto-resolution waits 20 s after a drop and raises only after 10 s of fast frames; people and animals hold a new animation 0.15 s before switching (no walk/run/idle flip-flopping); people keep their animated rig a little longer (no popping); camera collision pulls in fast and eases out; headbutt freeze (hit-stop) 75 → 40 ms. The version badge now shows the right number (v2 showed "v1"). Dev panel: shadow turn step, headbutt freeze.
- **v4** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v4.html` · save schema **s2** (new key `goat-mayhem/save/s2`; on first run v4 migrates the newest s1 save and leaves s1 untouched for v1–v3; s2 adds stats `lampsDown`, `treesDown`, `wallsDown`, `blocksDown`, `collapses`; launcher copy-progress has s1→s2 and s2→s1 converters). Destructible town (new `destruct.js`): ~4,700 breakable pieces — every building facade bay of every floor is a 0.3 m wall panel with its own collider and an inner side, floors are slabs you can walk on inside, roofs are pieces; every `solid()` (walls, stairs, statues, the water tower, barn, hedge maze…) is cut into ~3 m blocks; lamps, trees and bushes are pieces. Broken pieces are hidden by a per-vertex flag in the shader (main and shadow pass), their colliders switch off, and they throw rubble, glass and dust. Pieces rest on supports (panel: the three below it; slab/roof: 35 % of the floor below; blocks: whatever they stand on) and fall in a ripple when supports go; 60 % of a building's panels gone brings the rest down. Lamps and trees topple as real physics bodies; some rubble chunks are physical and kickable. Damage from headbutts, explosions and crashes (heavy, fast things). The town repairs itself out of sight after 120 s; Repair town button in the pause menu; dev panel: damage, rubble limits, repair timing, knock-down-nearby. 5 new tasks. Fixed: modules registered with `feature()` were never updated.
- **v5** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v5.html` · save schema **s2** (unchanged, shared with v4; extra players are not saved). Local multiplayer, co-op (shared chaos score, coins, tasks): 👥 2 players on the title screen and in pause adds a touch player; a spare controller pressing A joins (up to 4). Split screen with one camera per goat (landscape: side by side; portrait: top/bottom with the top half drawn upside down for a player across the iPad — "Face each other" toggle; 3–4 players: 2×2), per-player controls box turned to face its player, coloured collar and marker per goat, per-player pause, Swap sides. Touches belong to the player whose area they start in (one stick finger and one look finger per player; extra touches ignored). People and cars react to the nearest goat; culling and animated-people choice use every view; split screen uses blob shadows and no glow. A controller dropping pauses the game. Fixes: minimap skipped when hidden.
- **v6** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v6.html` · save schema **s2** (unchanged). Online play by room code (new `net.js`): PeerJS 1.5.5 (MIT, vendored as `vendor/peerjs-1.5.5.min.js`) over WebRTC, broker = PeerJS's free public cloud (no uptime guarantee; no TURN relay, so strict NATs / some mobile networks can't connect); `?peer=host:port` points at another broker (used by the tests). 5-character codes without look-alikes, invite link `?room=CODE` that fills the code, copy button; room ids `goat-mayhem-p2-CODE` (protocol 2). Host runs everything and applies the guest's actions (move/btn/hold); ~15 Hz snapshots of all goats and of moved props/people/cars within 85 m of the guest (spawn info sent once; `gone` list), guest glides between snapshots; events: piece breaks, repairs, explosions, chaos popups as text ids (each player sees their own language). Host's config applies to the guest (modded flag). Guest's people/cars/props are replaced by the host's while connected and its save isn't changed; guest leaving keeps the room open (rejoin with the same code); room full / wrong protocol ("your friend is on vX") refused with a message; host closing shows the guest a dialog back to the title; timeouts and broker errors show a message and a way back. Fix: one stuck code field after a failed join.
- **v7** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v7.html` · save schema **s2** (unchanged). Xbox fix (reported: the controller did nothing on the real Xbox): the page and the launcher set `navigator.gamepadInputEmulation = 'gamepad'` before anything else, as the hub and Drawrassic Park do, so Edge stops using the controller as its mouse cursor. On the Xbox (user agent contains "Xbox") a "Press A to start" card comes first: A as a controller button or as Edge's cursor click dismisses it, a click also turns on full screen; it shows how many controllers the page can see. L3+R3 switches between the game and Edge's cursor. Controller hints are on from the start on the Xbox. Launcher: same card, then the Continue button has focus.
- **v8** (2026-11-13) `goat-mayhem-v8.html` · save schema **s2** (unchanged). Performance and loading (reported: some stutter and slow loading on iPad): animated people and farm animals outside every view are hidden and not animated, far ones animate at half rate, empty instanced meshes are hidden (same view in headless Chrome: ~450 → ~150 draw calls; ~86 with the tablet profile); tablets/phones start with 18 animated people (was 32), 34 m rig distance and 4 hair layers (dev panel can raise them); the people/animal models start downloading before the town is built; shaders warm up with `compileAsync` (not blocking where the browser compiles in parallel); the goat sculpt evaluates only nearby shapes (≈1.3 s → 0.5 s in headless Chrome). People AI (reported: silly, stuck on obstacles): wander targets need a clear straight path (walls, trees, lamps at hip and chest height; not in water), look-ahead steering around walls, planters, parked cars, bins and crates, unstick by turning away and re-planning; stuck share of walking people over 60 s ~10 % → ~3 %, nobody wanders into the pond. New behaviour: dodge a goat charging at them, stop to watch it (wave, cheer when it is in the air, photo if they carry a camera), chat in pairs. Dev panel: look-ahead, longest walk, watch/dodge distances, chat chance. `__boot` load-time breakdown for tests. 3D start-up (reported on Xbox Edge: "Error creating WebGL context"): the 3D context is opened before the town is built and tried six ways (WebGL 2 with/without anti-aliasing, WebGL 1, low-power) on fresh canvases, with the browser's reasons kept; if none works, a friendly screen in 4 languages explains it (on Xbox: quit Edge fully, restart the console, run the 3D check, or play on the iPad with the controller over Bluetooth), gives Edge its cursor back and offers Try again / 3D check / Back (also by controller). If the 3D context is lost mid-game the game saves and shows a Reload screen. Xbox profile: pixel ratio 1, 1024 shadow map, no MSAA buffer, 4 hair layers, 20 animated people. WebGL 1 fix: three r160 skinning needs WebGL 2, so on WebGL 1 skeletons use a fixed 32×32 bone texture read with texture2D. New `webgl-check.html` (linked from the launcher): reports WebGL 2/1 availability, graphics chip, max texture, draws a spinning cube; never touches saves. Fix: nearby-body lookups used stale indices after the body list changed (could crash people's steering). Fix (since v5): unplugging player 1's controller now pauses the game. Shader warm-up uses `compile()` (three's `compileAsync` polling threw when a 2nd player joined).
