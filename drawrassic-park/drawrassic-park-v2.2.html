🛒 Park Shop

🦖 Welcome to Drawrassic Park!

  • ✏️ Draw a Dino — draw it facing right →, pick what it eats and how it moves, then open the gates.
  • 🍖 Meat / 🥬 Plants — tap the park to drop food. Drop meat in the lake for swimmers.
  • 👆 Watch — tap a dino to see its stats. Drag a dino to pick it up and move it.
  • ✋ Pet — rub dinos to make them happy. Petting also breaks up fights.
  • Meat-eaters hunt critters and plant-eaters; brave dinos fight back. Knocked-out dinos rest, then get back up.
  • Well-fed, happy dinos at level 3+ may lay eggs 🥚 — tap a wobbling egg to hatch it.
  • Tap amber gems to collect them, then spend them in the 🛒 Shop.
  • 🌑 At night, small shadow dinos sneak in and attack. Your dinos fight back. Tap a shadow to blind it with a flash. Defeated shadows drop 🪙 coins.
  • 📜 Spend coins on new dinosaur blueprints in the Shop, then draw them in ✏️ Draw a Dino.
  • 💬 Tap a dino to hear what it's thinking.
  • 💾 Tap Save while drawing to keep a dino in your 📦 Dino Box. Release it into the park any time. Set a park limit in ⚙️ Settings (no limit by default).
